About Early Steps Therapy

Where little steps become big milestones

Early Steps Therapy offers pediatric physical therapy services for children ages 0–21 at our newly renovated facility.

Every child moves, plays, and learns in their own way — so every plan of care here is built around your child, not a template. Sessions are hands-on, playful, and focused on the goals that matter to your family.

How we work

Therapeutic techniques we use

Our therapists draw on proven, specialized approaches — matched to what helps your child progress best.

CME (MEDEK)

Cuevas Medek Exercises challenge a child's automatic postural responses to provoke new gross motor skills.

Learn more →

NDT

Neuro-Developmental Treatment uses hands-on guidance to improve posture, movement quality, and function.

Learn more →

Kinesio Taping

Elastic therapeutic taping that supports muscles and joints while keeping full freedom of movement.

Learn more →

Conductive Education

A holistic, task-based learning approach that builds independence in everyday movement.

Learn more →

E-stim, Manual Therapy & Myofascial Release

Targeted hands-on techniques and gentle electrical stimulation to activate muscles and release tightness.
